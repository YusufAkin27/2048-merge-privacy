English

2048 Merge: Number Puzzle ("the Game") is a single-player puzzle game developed by Akin Studios. This policy explains what the Game does with data.

Data we collect

The Game does not collect, transmit to any server, or share any personal data. It does not create accounts, contains no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Game progress, scores, statistics and achievementsSo you can continue where you left off and see your recordsOnly on your device
Settings (theme, language, sound, vibration)To remember your preferencesOnly on your device

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or use "Reset progress" in Settings.

Permissions

  • Vibration – for optional haptic feedback. It can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
